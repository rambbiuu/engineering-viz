    Glossary
    Fundamental
    The base sine wave, one cycle per repeat of the target. Every other ingredient is a multiple of it.
    Harmonic n
    A sine wave at n times the fundamental frequency.
    Coefficient, bn
    How much of harmonic n the recipe calls for. A negative value means the sine goes in upside down.
    Fourier series
    The recipe itself: the full list of coefficients that rebuilds the repeating wave exactly.
    Spectrum
    The recipe drawn as a bar chart, one bar per harmonic. Section 3 is a spectrum.
    Gibbs effect
    The overshoot spikes beside any sharp jump. Adding more harmonics makes them narrower but never shorter — about 9 % overshoot remains.
    RMS
    Root mean square — an average size for a wiggly difference, ignoring its sign.
