Noise, and the weakest signal a receiver can hear

Every resistor above absolute zero makes noise, and every stage of a receiver adds its own on top. That floor is what decides how faint a signal you can still pull out — and the first amplifier decides almost all of it.

Everything here is in decibels: +3 dB is twice the power, −3 dB is half, +10 dB is ten times. dBm is the same scale measured from one milliwatt.
