The arithmetic, with the current numbers
    Everyday version, and what the sliders teach

    Shout across a canyon and the echo arrives while you are still speaking, so syllables overlap and turn to mush. Radio echoes arrive microseconds late and cancel parts of the signal outright.

    Delay spread and Doppler are independent axes of the same channel. Delay spread is about distance: it sets the coherence bandwidth, and once the signal is wider than that, no transmit power helps — the fix is an equaliser, a rake receiver, or narrow subcarriers as OFDM uses. Doppler is about speed: it sets how fast the pattern sweeps past, so a 30 dB fade at walking pace loses a whole packet while the same fade at 200 km/h is over in a millisecond. A large K factor shrinks both, which is why line of sight is worth more than the link budget alone suggests.

    Two honest limits. The envelope is the exact vector sum of the handful of paths set here, so its statistics only approach Rayleigh as taps are added. The outage, crossing rate and fade duration cards use the classical Rayleigh formulas, which assume many equal scatterers from all directions; the outage footnote also reports what the drawn trace actually did.

    Glossary
    Multipath
    The signal arriving by several routes at once, after reflecting off buildings, ground or hills.
    Tap
    One arriving copy, described by its delay, its amplitude and its phase.
    Mean excess delay, τ̄
    The power-weighted average of the arrival delays, measured from the first copy.
    RMS delay spread, στ
    The power-weighted standard deviation of the arrival delays. One number for how smeared in time the channel is.
    Coherence bandwidth, Bc
    The width in frequency over which the channel gain stays roughly constant: 1/(5στ) for 0.5 correlation, 1/(50στ) for 0.9.
    Flat fading
    The signal is narrower than Bc, so all of it fades by the same amount at the same moment.
    Frequency-selective fading
    The signal is wider than Bc, so some frequencies inside it are destroyed while others survive. This causes inter-symbol interference.
    Doppler shift, fd
    The shift a moving receiver sees on one path, (v/λ)cosθ, with θ between the path and the direction of travel.
    Maximum Doppler, fm
    v/λ, the shift on a path arriving from straight ahead. It sets how fast the fading pattern changes.
    Coherence time, Tc
    How long the channel stays roughly the same, about 0.423/fm.
    Rayleigh fading
    The envelope statistics when many similar copies arrive and none dominates. Deep fades are common.
    Rician K factor
    Power in the dominant direct path over power in everything scattered, A²/2σ², quoted in dB.
    Fade margin
    Headroom in decibels above the level at which the link stops working.
    Outage
    The fraction of time the received level sits below that threshold.
    Level crossing rate
    How many times per second the envelope crosses the threshold going downwards.
    Average fade duration
    The mean length of one of those dropouts.
