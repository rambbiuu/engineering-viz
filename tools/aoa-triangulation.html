AoA triangulation

One DF station — like your 4-antenna interferometer — gives a direction, not a position: the emitter is somewhere along that bearing line. Two stations give two lines, and lines cross at exactly one place. That crossing is the fix.

Play with the geometry: with the emitter far away, even ±2° wedges overlap in a huge diamond — angle error grows into position error with distance. Move the emitter between the two stations and the wedges cross nearly at right angles: tight fix. Directly above the line joining the stations, the wedges cross at a shallow angle and the diamond stretches badly. Real systems add a third station or fly a baseline for exactly this reason.
