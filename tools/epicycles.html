The recipe, with the current numbers
    Glossary
    Epicycle
    A circle whose centre rides on the rim of another circle. Ancient astronomers used stacks of them to trace planet paths; here the stack traces a wave.
    Fundamental
    The biggest, slowest circle — one turn per repeat of the shape. Every other circle spins at a whole-number multiple of its speed.
    Harmonic
    A circle spinning at n times the fundamental's speed. The trace it adds is a sine wave at n times the base frequency.
    Amplitude
    A circle's radius. It says how much of that harmonic the target shape needs.
    Phase
    Where on its circle an arm starts. A negative recipe entry starts the arm half a turn round.
    Fourier series
    The recipe itself: the list of speeds and radii that rebuilds any repeating shape from plain sine waves. The bar chart in the builder tool and this circle stack are the same list drawn two ways.

    A sharp edge needs many fast circles — the square's come only in odd speeds (1×, 3×, 5×…), the sawtooth uses every speed, and a sharp edge in time always means a long tail in frequency.
