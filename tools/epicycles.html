Spinning circles draw your wave

One point riding a spinning circle traces a perfect sine in time. Stack more circles — each smaller and spinning faster, tip to tip — and the last tip traces squares, triangles, sawtooths. Every repeating shape is just circles riding circles: the same fact as the Fourier series, seen with your own eyes.

4

Watch how the stack behaves: for the sine one circle is already perfect. The square needs many circles — and they only come in odd speeds (1×, 3×, 5×…), each smaller by that factor. The sawtooth uses every speed. Slide the circle count up and the trace sharpens; this is literally the Fourier series builder from the other tool, with the recipe bars replaced by spinning radii.
