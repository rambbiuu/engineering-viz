The recipe, with the current numbers
    Glossary
    Epicycle
    A circle whose centre rides on the rim of another circle.
    Fundamental
    The biggest, slowest circle — one turn per repeat of the shape.
    Harmonic
    A circle spinning at n times the fundamental speed.
    Amplitude
    A circle’s radius: how much of that harmonic the shape needs.
    Phase
    Where on its circle an arm starts. A negative recipe entry starts it half a turn round.
    Gibbs ringing
    A fixed overshoot beside a jump that never shrinks, however many circles you add.
    Fourier series
    The list of speeds and radii that rebuilds any repeating shape from sines.

    A sharp edge in time always means a long tail in frequency: the square uses odd speeds only, the sawtooth uses every speed.
