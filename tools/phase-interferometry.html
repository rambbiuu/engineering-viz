Phase interferometry

One equation runs everything here: Δφ = 360° · d · sinθ / λ, wrapped to ±180°. Antennas on one line, spaced unequally, turn that into an angle of arrival. Short spacings are honest but coarse; long ones are precise but hide whole cycles, and the short ones are what tell you how many are hidden.

4 · Phase difference vs emitter angle — sawtooth jumps = wrapping; dots = what the cards read right now

5 · Geolocation mode — phases in, angle out

The inverse direction, the one a real receiver runs: from the wrapped phases above plus the measured frequency, recover the angle by adding the hidden cycles back one baseline at a time.
