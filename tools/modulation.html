About this scheme
—
Worked example
FM at a 20 Hz carrier with 6 Hz deviation: the message swings the instantaneous frequency from 20 − 6 = 14 Hz up to 20 + 6 = 26 Hz. QPSK sending 8 bits packs them into 8 ÷ 2 = 4 symbols, so it finishes in half the time BPSK needs.
The family resemblance
The analog schemes (AM, FM) copy a smooth message onto the carrier; the digital ones (OOK, FSK, BPSK) snap between discrete states per bit. OOK is extreme AM, FSK is FM with only two frequencies, and BPSK — flipping phase 180° per bit — is the move an interferometer measures in reverse: it reads phase, BPSK writes it.
Glossary
Carrier
The high-frequency wave that transports the message.
Amplitude
The height of the wave.
Frequency
How many wave cycles fit in one second.
Phase
Where the wave is in its cycle at a given instant.
Envelope
The outline traced by the wave's peaks.
Keying
Digital modulation: snapping between discrete states, one per bit or symbol.
